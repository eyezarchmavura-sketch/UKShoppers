# Public eBay Interaction Patterns — Design Reference Notes

**Reference reviewed:** https://www.ebay.co.uk/ on 13 August 2026.

## Observed patterns to adapt, not replicate

The public eBay UK homepage makes discovery immediate with a persistent header search field, a category selector, a clear search action, and a secondary horizontal category rail. It also uses promotional modules and a trust-focused hero to direct users into product exploration. Its homepage includes account, notification, and basket affordances, but these map only partially to UK Shoppers Africa because this product is a cross-border shopping and fulfilment service rather than a marketplace that sells inventory directly.

## Original UK Shoppers Africa translation

1. Add a clear **“Find a UK store”** discovery field and category selector that route to the existing Store Directory rather than implying an internal product catalogue.
2. Add a compact category rail for the supported-retailer catalogue, keeping the categories rooted in the project’s verified store list.
3. Replace retail discount promotion patterns with a truthful, trust-led **Service Promise** strip: staff-reviewed quote, item verification at the UK warehouse, and local delivery updates.
4. Maintain separate actions for **opening the retailer site** and **requesting staff review**. UK Shoppers Africa must not claim to own the retailer product catalogue or checkout.
5. Avoid copying eBay names, visual assets, product content, or brand language. Implement only general interaction concepts in the UK Shoppers Africa gold/black/blue system.
