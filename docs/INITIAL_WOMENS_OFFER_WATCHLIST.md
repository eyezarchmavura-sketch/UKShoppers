# Initial Women-First Retailer Watchlist

**Status:** Research and operating guide only. This file does not authorise publication of an offer, coupon, price, or discount. Every customer-facing offer must still be checked and approved in the staff workspace.

## Recommended launch focus

The first watchlist should make UK Shoppers Africa immediately useful to its predominantly women customer base while remaining small enough to verify quickly. The priority is women’s fashion, footwear, skincare, haircare, makeup, fragrance, bags, and occasionwear. Sportswear, department stores, and general marketplaces should be treated as support sources rather than leading messages.

## Evidence observed during source review

The ASOS homepage publicly routes shoppers to its **Women** area, women’s brands, and a Black Friday information link. The LOOKFANTASTIC homepage publicly exposes an **Offers** area alongside makeup, hair, skincare, fragrance, gifting, and luxury sections. Its page also displays time-sensitive product claims and campaign code language, which confirms why staff must capture the source URL, terms, and verification time before publishing—not copy an unverified headline into the UK Shoppers Africa app. [1] [2]

The official Superdrug Top Offers page exposes dedicated offers, fashion, Korean and Japanese beauty, premium beauty, gifts, and marketplace navigation. It also links to customer terms, including Health & Beautycard and marketplace terms; this makes it a strong value-beauty watchlist source, but the team must distinguish retailer offers from marketplace conditions before publishing. The Boots source was reachable through its official beauty and skincare offers route, but did not render in the research browser; its result is therefore recorded as a staff-check entry point rather than as evidence of a current customer-facing offer. [3] [4]

## Staff source-check routine

The operations team should begin each weekday by checking the priority sources in order. A verified offer may be published only when the staff member records the official retailer link, visible offer terms, an expiry date or a documented “while stocks last” limitation, the intended customer destination, and the verification time. If any condition is missing, it remains a staff research note rather than a public promotion.

The initial content rhythm should be a small number of high-confidence offer cards rather than a crowded deal feed: two to four verified fashion or footwear highlights and two to four beauty, skincare, or haircare highlights. This protects the team’s credibility, lets Queen reference only real current offers, and keeps the hero section useful even during quieter sales periods.

## Customer-facing wording

Use **“Verified UK offers for women’s fashion and beauty”** as the section title. Each card should show the retailer, the offer statement exactly as confirmed, a “Verified [time/date]” label, expiry or stock wording, short terms, and a button that takes the shopper to the official retailer page before they bring the link or cart back for staff review.

Do not use “lowest price,” “exclusive,” “guaranteed saving,” or a crossed-out reference price unless the official source and comparison basis support it. If a partner or affiliate link is used, disclose it near the link.
