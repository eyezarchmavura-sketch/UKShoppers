# UK Shoppers Africa — Client Pricing Recommendation

**Prepared:** 14 August 2026  
**Prepared by:** Manus AI  
**Commercial position:** Bespoke cross-border-shopping platform, not a brochure website

> **Important:** This is commercial decision support, not legal, tax, accounting, or regulated financial advice. Confirm the final scope, applicable taxes, processor fees, currency treatment, refund responsibilities, and contract terms with the client and suitable professional advisers before signing.

## Executive recommendation

> Quote **£12,500** as the fixed delivery price for the current, documented platform scope, and sell a **£750 per month Operations & Growth Care Plan** after launch. Third-party platform, domain, email, payment-processing, messaging, and provider charges should be billed directly to the client or passed through at cost under a written agreement.

This is a defensible price because UK Shoppers Africa is a custom operational platform rather than a template site. It already combines a multilingual public site, authenticated customer portal, secure request intake, real order and payment data structures, staff operations queue, notification pathways, shipment-status views, retailer discovery, and an AI assistant. The recommended price is deliberately below the upper range for comparable custom applications to support an early client relationship while still reflecting the accountability and specialist delivery required.

| Commercial item | Recommended price | Use it when | Commercial intent |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Current platform delivery** | **£12,500 fixed** | The client buys the completed documented scope below | Position it as a bespoke launch-ready platform, excluding live payment-provider activation and third-party fees |
| **Operations & Growth Care Plan** | **£750/month** | Recommended post-launch operating relationship | Funds active maintenance, delivery support, controlled improvements, and reporting |
| Stripe live-payment activation | **£1,750 fixed add-on** | After the client completes verification and approves checkout products, refunds, and webhook operations | Covers server-side Checkout, webhook hardening, test-to-live validation, and handover—not Stripe processing fees |
| Safaricom Kenya or Vodacom Tanzania activation | **£2,500 per provider fixed add-on** | After the provider credentials, callback requirements, and commercial terms are approved | Covers provider-specific integration, callback validation, and test evidence |
| New feature work outside the plan | **£75/hour** or separately quoted fixed scope | New dashboards, store APIs, mobile applications, or automation | Prevents support retainers from becoming unlimited development contracts |

The £12,500 delivery plus £750/month plan produces a **first-year service value of £21,500**, excluding pass-through costs and optional payment integrations. The calculation is £12,500 + (12 × £750).

## What the fixed price includes

The fixed delivery price covers the current deployed scope, namely the responsive gold/black/blue public storefront; English, Kiswahili, Kinyarwanda, and Luganda support; verified-store discovery and category search; authenticated customer accounts; purchase-request intake with delivery addresses; customer order and payment history; staff-safe operations queue; notification and email preferences; Queen’s customer-assistance interface; basic legal pages; database-backed records; and the documented operational runbook.

It also covers reasonable launch documentation, a handover session, and a **30-day defect warranty** for failures in the agreed released scope. A defect warranty means correcting a reproducible fault in delivered functionality; it does not include new features, changed designs, new integrations, new content production, or business-process changes.

## What the fixed price excludes

The quote should be explicit about exclusions. Live Stripe, Safaricom, Vodacom, MTN MoMo, and Airtel Money activation remain separate because each requires the client’s verified account, approved commercial terms, exact callback configuration, and payment policy. It also excludes payment-processing fees, chargebacks, transaction reversals, customs duties, shipping fees, retailer charges, paid advertising, translation by a certified professional, support staffing, copywriting beyond the existing site, and fulfilment operations.

Do not promise that a payment provider will approve the business, that a card or mobile-money transaction will succeed, or that the website will produce a particular level of sales. Those outcomes depend on the client’s compliance, provider relationship, logistics, customer service, pricing, and marketing.

## Monthly operating-service options

| Plan | Monthly price | Suitable client stage | Included service boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Launch Care** | **£350/month** | First months after a modest launch | Availability and error review; routine dependency/security checks; monthly backup/recovery review; up to 2 hours of small content or configuration changes; response target of 2 business days |
| **Operations & Growth Care — recommended** | **£750/month** | Active customer portal with regular order activity | All Launch Care items; weekly operational review; monitoring of errors and payment/webhook alerts; monthly KPI and backlog review; up to 6 hours of controlled small improvements; response target of 1 business day |
| **Priority Partner Care** | **£1,250/month** | Higher-volume or campaign-driven operation | All Operations & Growth items; weekday priority response target of 4 business hours; biweekly improvement planning; up to 12 hours of planned work; release coordination and quarterly recovery drill |

Each plan should use a rolling three-month minimum term and state that unused hours do not roll forward. Out-of-scope work is billed at £75/hour only after written approval. A separate support contract should define client contacts, support hours, incident severity, acceptance criteria, data responsibilities, and who may approve scope changes.

## Pass-through operating costs

The client should own or directly pay for essential third-party accounts wherever possible. This protects both parties if the relationship ends and makes the client’s real operating cost transparent.

| Cost category | Recommended billing treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Domain, DNS, and business email | Client-owned account; pass through at cost | Avoids ownership disputes and keeps renewal visible |
| Hosting, database, storage, and monitoring | Client-owned account or at-cost pass-through | Usage changes with traffic and data growth |
| Payment-processor charges, refunds, and chargebacks | Client pays provider directly | These depend on the provider, country, card mix, risk decisions, and transaction volume |
| WhatsApp, SMS, email, AI, and other usage-based services | Client-owned account or at-cost pass-through | Consumption is variable and should not silently erode the care-plan margin |
| Customs, shipping, retailer purchases, and fulfilment | Client operational expense | These are trading costs, not website-maintenance costs |

Published UK guidance places custom web applications in a materially higher bracket than basic brochure sites: one 2026 guide cites **£15,000–£50,000+** for custom applications, while a global agency survey places the most common custom web application and portal band at **$20,000–$50,000**. The same survey reports that most monthly retainers sit between **$500 and $3,000**. These are market benchmarks, not quotations for this project; they support the fact that a £12,500 build and £750 monthly plan are commercially moderate for the functionality delivered.[1] [2]

## Client-facing payment schedule

Use a milestone schedule that protects the work while giving the client clear acceptance points.

| Milestone | Share | Invoice amount | Acceptance point |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Contract signature and project reservation | 40% | £5,000 | Scope, responsibilities, and access agreed |
| Staging review and acceptance | 35% | £4,375 | Agreed customer and staff workflows demonstrated |
| Production handover | 25% | £3,125 | Domain, documentation, and agreed launch checklist complete |

The recurring care plan should begin after the 30-day defect-warranty period, unless the client elects to start it immediately for launch support. Payment-provider add-ons should be invoiced separately: 50% at integration start and 50% after the agreed test evidence and handover are complete.

## Suggested wording for the proposal

> **UK Shoppers Africa is a custom cross-border shopping operations platform for East African customers buying from UK retailers.** The £12,500 delivery investment covers the current multilingual storefront, retailer discovery, customer portal, staff operations workflow, order tracking, notification foundation, and secure database-backed request journey. It excludes third-party transaction and provider costs, which remain transparent client expenses. After launch, the recommended £750/month Operations & Growth Care Plan keeps the platform monitored, maintained, and improved within a clear monthly service boundary.

## Why this is the right commercial posture

Avoid pricing this as “a website.” That phrase invites comparison with a simple marketing page and ignores the operational value: reducing order ambiguity, giving customers a request and tracking path, enabling staff workflow control, and making the service more trustworthy across East African markets. Sell the system as a **revenue-supporting operations platform** and separate three money flows: the initial intellectual-property and implementation fee; monthly availability and improvement service; and client-owned third-party operating costs.

For a relationship-led opening offer, do not drop the whole build below **£9,500** or the monthly plan below **£350** without reducing scope. Below that level, remove either multilingual support, the customer portal, AI assistance, the staff queue, or ongoing support commitments rather than silently absorbing the cost. Conversely, if the client needs live payments, more than one country-specific money provider, 24/7 support, product APIs, or a mobile app, quote those as separately scoped work.
