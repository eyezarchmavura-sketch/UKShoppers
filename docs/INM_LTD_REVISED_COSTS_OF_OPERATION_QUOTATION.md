## 2. Domain and Hosting Position

The existing **UK Shoppers Africa** domain will be reused for the new platform. Therefore, this quotation contains **no charge for purchasing or registering a new domain**. The domain-connection work and the initial 12-month standard hosting provision are included in the allocation above.

If the existing domain later reaches its renewal date and has not already been renewed by its owner, the renewal cost will be disclosed in writing before any payment is requested. No new domain will be purchased unless the business expressly approves it in writing.

## 4. Included Scope — No Additional INM LTD Charges Within This Scope

The TZS 2,500,000 total covers the six workstreams in Section 1. It does **not** create a separate charge for a new domain, ordinary deployment work, activation preparation, the stated handover session, or the initial 30-day post-launch support period. INM LTD will not add further implementation charges for these stated items.

> **Change-control commitment:** any request that is outside this stated scope will first be described, priced where necessary, and submitted for written approval. It will not be treated as an automatic additional cost.

## 5. Items Not Included Unless Separately Approved in Writing

The following are excluded because they depend on a third-party provider, usage level, or a future business decision. They are not hidden charges within the TZS 2,500,000 total.

| Excluded or optional item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Payment-provider transaction fees, bank charges, and currency-conversion charges | Charged directly by the relevant provider or bank according to its own terms. |
| Live WhatsApp Business, SMS, email, or other messaging-provider usage fees | Require provider configuration, approved credentials/templates where applicable, and written approval before activation. |
| Live payment-gateway account verification, recurring provider charges, or premium third-party licences | Activated only after the business chooses the provider and approves its terms. |
| New features, additional applications, new country operations, substantial redesigns, or integrations outside the scope above | Separately scoped and priced only after written approval. |
| Hosting and maintenance after the included initial periods | Any renewal, resource upgrade, or continuing retainer will be proposed and agreed separately in writing before it becomes payable. |

## 6. Service Limits and Next Step

The initial post-launch support period covers correction of defects and minor configuration help within the delivered scope. It does not include unlimited feature development, provider fees, or unrelated business services. A longer maintenance, hosting, or growth-support arrangement can be discussed after activation; no monthly retainer is fixed by this quotation.

If the allocation and scope are acceptable, INM LTD can record the written confirmation and proceed with the agreed activation steps after the outstanding operational balance is addressed. If any line item needs adjustment before confirmation, it can be revised transparently first.
