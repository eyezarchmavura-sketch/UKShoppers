# UK Deals Reference Findings — 26 August 2026

The supplied legacy UK Shoppers Africa site was reviewed as a **reference for customer needs, not as a source to copy**. Its `/uk-deals/` page currently exposes the title and site navigation but no visible deal cards or product merchandising in the extracted customer view.[1] The related shop page shows a conventional catalogue model: category filters, brand filters, price filtering, sorting, pagination, and a 158-item product catalogue.[2] The category page foregrounds Fashion, Fragrance, Hair Care, Health, Shoes, and Skin Care.[3]

| Reference pattern | What it confirms | UK Shoppers Africa response |
|---|---|---|
| Product-led visual discovery | The audience expects browseable shopping categories, especially fashion, shoes, hair care, and skin care. | Replace the homepage questions-and-answers block with a women-first visual discovery section that routes to supported stores and the staff-reviewed request flow. |
| Category and brand filters | Shoppers value clear ways to narrow a large catalogue. | Use concise visual category pathways rather than duplicating the prior site's catalogue or product data. |
| Deals navigation | Customers expect a dedicated place to find savings. | Keep a distinct homepage verified-deals panel: only official/approved retailer source records with terms and dated availability can appear. |
| Manual quote and forwarding journey | The reference explains both assisted shopping and self-shopping with parcel forwarding.[4] | Preserve UK Shoppers Africa's stronger customer portal, staff-reviewed request intake, operations queue, and evidence-based offer controls. |

The legacy source lists individual product titles, brands, prices, and category structure. None of that product catalogue, customer information, pricing, or marketing copy will be copied into this project. The implementation will use original content, visual category storytelling, approved retailer destinations, and only source-checked promotion records.

## Official retailer deal destinations selected for the homepage

The homepage can safely help customers reach retailer-owned deal pages without restating a volatile price, percentage, coupon, stock position, or eligibility condition. The customer must see the retailer’s own current terms when the external page opens. The selected destinations below were reviewed on 26 August 2026 and are intentionally not converted into UK Shoppers Africa product listings.

| Retailer | Customer destination | Product emphasis | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASOS | Women’s Sale | Women’s fashion, shoes and accessories | Link to the retailer-owned sale category; do not repeat its changing product prices or sale percentages. |
| LOOKFANTASTIC | Beauty Offers | Skincare, haircare, fragrance and makeup | Link to the retailer-owned offers hub; the retailer controls availability and terms. |
| Superdrug | Top Offers | Makeup, skincare and haircare | Link to the retailer-owned top-offers hub; no coupon claim is shown by UK Shoppers Africa. |
| Marks & Spencer | Womenswear Sale | Clothing, dresses and accessories | Link to the retailer-owned womenswear sale page; stock and offer terms remain retailer-controlled. |
