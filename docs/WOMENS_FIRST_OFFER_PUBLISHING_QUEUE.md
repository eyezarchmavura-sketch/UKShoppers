# Women-First Offer Verification & Publishing Queue

**Purpose:** Give the UK Shoppers Africa operations team a disciplined way to surface timely UK savings for predominantly women customers without publishing stale, misleading, or unsupported promotions.

> This is a research and operational queue, not a list of live offers. An entry becomes public only after a staff member completes the verification fields in `/admin/offers` and publishes it.

## Launch message

The customer-facing offer area should lead with **Women’s Fashion & Beauty: Verified UK Savings**. This covers the purchasing needs most likely to recur: women’s clothing, shoes, bags, occasionwear, skincare, haircare, makeup, fragrance, and gifting. Sport and general retail offers can appear later when they satisfy the same evidence and relevance standard.

## Required verification sequence

The assigned staff member opens the official UK retailer page and identifies one specific campaign, product group, or code. They must not copy an offer from a social-media post, deal aggregator, or memory. They record the official source URL, terms summary, audience/restrictions, offer expiry, intended customer link, and any material limitations before saving it as a draft.

A second staff member, or the same staff member after a deliberate re-check, confirms that the source still shows the offer. The team then publishes through `/admin/offers`. The system records the publish time and verifier server-side. If the offer has no future expiry, has unclear conditions, does not serve women-first demand, or does not work from the UK source, it is not published.

## Customer card standard

Each published card should state the retailer, a restrained offer headline, the exact short terms, the expiry/stock qualifier, the “Verified” date and time, and a button to the official retailer page. Where a tracked partner link is used, the card must state that it may be a partner link. The customer should then return with the product link or cart screenshot for a staff-reviewed request—never assume that overseas eligibility, stock, or shipping is guaranteed.

## Operating cadence

Morning monitoring should focus on ASOS, LOOKFANTASTIC, Boots, and Superdrug. A midday re-check removes expired or changed cards. Before high-intent periods such as Black Friday, Christmas, Eid, Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, and wedding season, the team increases source checks for the most relevant retailer categories but still publishes only verified terms.

The hero should normally show no more than six current offers. This gives customers speed without creating a noisy coupon wall and keeps operations able to update every visible offer promptly. During quiet periods, the truthful empty state is preferable to outdated savings claims.

## Handover trigger for future automation

When the team has shown that it can maintain this queue accurately for four weeks, the next safe investment is approved affiliate-network or retailer data feeds. Automation should ingest data as **drafts**, not public offers, so staff retain the final verification and publication decision.
