## 1. Quotation purpose

This statement records the professional delivery value of the **UK Shoppers Africa** digital shopping and parcel-forwarding system, and credits the payment received to date. The platform has been designed as a working customer and operations system for UK-to-East-Africa shopping rather than a basic information website.[1]

The figures above use the documented fixed delivery price of **£12,500.00**. The Tanzanian-shilling amounts are calculated using a reference rate of **TZS 3,585.6286 for £1.00**, accessed on 23 August 2026.[2] The final settlement currency, rate date, and payment method should be confirmed in writing before payment, because foreign-exchange rates and bank charges may change.

## 2. Delivered platform scope

The quoted delivery scope includes the completed UK Shoppers Africa platform foundation:

| Delivered area | Included value |
|---|---|
| Customer experience | Public UK-store discovery, customer account portal, multilingual English/Kiswahili/Kinyarwanda/Luganda experience, and guided purchase-request flow. |
| Purchase operations | Product-link and cart-screenshot intake, delivery-address capture, customer order records, staff-reviewed quote workflow, and order-status tracking foundation. |
| Staff control | Role-restricted operations queue, external staff invitations, controlled customer-request review, and operations documentation. |
| Customer communication | Queen assistance features, in-app updates, email-notification foundation, payment history, receipts, and legal-information pages. |
| Technical foundation | Database-backed records, secure role controls, documentation, testing, and an architecture prepared for approved payment and messaging providers. |

## 3. Costs intentionally excluded from this quotation

The amount due above is the **platform delivery balance only**. It does not include charges that must be approved, paid, or invoiced separately, such as payment-processing fees, mobile-money provider fees, shipping, customs, retailer purchases, domain and hosting renewal, external messaging, AI usage, advertising, bank fees, currency conversion, refunds, or future features outside the documented delivery scope.[1]

Live activation of Stripe, Safaricom/Vodacom mobile money, and WhatsApp Business is not included in the balance until the relevant business accounts, credentials, and provider terms are formally approved.
