Thank you for the collaboration on the **UK Shoppers Africa** digital platform. The work has been developed as a cross-border shopping operations system for East African customers purchasing from UK retailers—not as a simple brochure website. This letter transparently records the professional delivery value of the system, the payment received to date, the outstanding balance, and the operating value the platform is designed to provide to the business.

## 1. Delivered system scope and business value

The quoted delivery scope covers the current custom platform: a multilingual customer-facing storefront; English, Kiswahili, Kinyarwanda, and Luganda support; supported-store discovery and search; customer accounts; purchase-request intake with delivery addresses; customer order and payment history; a role-restricted staff operations queue; shipment and notification foundations; Queen customer-assistance features; core legal information; database-backed records; and an operations handover guide.[1]

The system is intended to reduce the manual uncertainty that often occurs in personal-shopping and parcel-forwarding services. It gives a customer a clear route to submit a UK item request, receive a staff-reviewed quote, follow an order journey, and receive updates. For the business, it provides a structured operational record, controlled staff access, and a foundation for payment, messaging, and reporting integrations when the relevant provider accounts and credentials are approved.

The commercial delivery figure below is therefore a **professional implementation quote for the completed platform scope**, rather than a claim that every amount was spent as an out-of-pocket cash expense. Third-party items such as hosting, domain renewals, payment-processing charges, shipping, customs, messaging, and provider fees remain client-owned or chargeable at cost under a separate written agreement.[1]

## 4. Services and costs outside this balance

The following are deliberately excluded from the delivery balance unless they are separately approved in writing: live activation of Stripe or country-specific mobile-money providers; payment-processing fees; refunds and chargebacks; bank or currency-conversion fees; shipping, customs, retailer, and fulfilment charges; domain, hosting, storage, messaging, AI, and email usage charges; paid advertising; and new features outside the agreed delivery scope.[1]

After the initial delivery period, the recommended ongoing service is the **Operations & Growth Care Plan at £750 per month** (approximately TZS 2,689,221.45 at the same reference rate). This is optional and separate from the balance above. It covers scheduled platform care, monitoring, controlled small improvements, and operational reviews within its stated service boundary; it does not include unlimited development work or third-party charges.[1]

## 5. Proposed fair completion and handover process

To protect both the business and the work already completed, I recommend that the parties confirm the following in writing before final settlement:

1. Confirm the documented delivered scope and any features that are expressly deferred, especially live payment-provider activation and WhatsApp Business setup.
2. Confirm the remaining delivery balance of **TZS 44,320,357.50** on the stated reference-rate basis, or agree a revised settlement-currency and rate-date in writing.
3. Agree the handover milestones, authorised administrators, source-code or account ownership, and the treatment of any external services.
4. Confirm whether the optional monthly Operations & Growth Care Plan will begin after handover and the initial defect-warranty period.

This approach gives the company a clear record of what has been built, what has been paid, what remains payable, and which costs must remain transparent rather than being mixed into the platform delivery price.
